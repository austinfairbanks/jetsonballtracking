## Decisions and constraints

- Detector should independently reacquire across the whole image.
- Temporal state should include confidence, elapsed time and lost/uncertain status; motion must not override strong current visual evidence.
- Camera motion must be separated from ball motion. A detector plus a simple motion filter is a useful baseline before a learned temporal model.
